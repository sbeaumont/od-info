How the Networth Tracker works

Networth is a rough calculation of the overall strength of a dominion. Although not precise, it is useful in trend tracking.

Two useful trends

Who is growing fastest
The Top {{ vm.top_bottom_count }} is a threat forming. Networth climbs when a dominion explores, builds and trains, so the dominions at the top of that list are the ones converting a lead into more of a lead. If one of them is in your range, that is the one to keep a Clear Sight on.
Who has stopped
Unchanged NW is the opposite. A dominion whose networth has not moved all period is one nobody is playing. An inactive dominion has probably let Ares Call lapse, and its defence is whatever it was last time somebody logged in. That makes it a target. Muhaha.
The Bottom list was added for completeness' sake, but is basically tells you who got hit, which you can also see in the Town Crier. What this does tell you who got hurt the most.

Readings come from the search screen

The tracker never reads a Clear Sight, a Barracks Spy or anything else you had to spend mana or spies on. Every number on the page comes off the game's own search page, which lists every dominion in the round with its land and its networth.

Every time you update, the search page is read and one row per dominion is stored. That row is a reading. A delta is the difference between the oldest and the newest reading inside the period you picked, so it takes two readings before there is anything to report.

A dominion with only one reading in the period is left out of all three tables. It is not unchanged, it is unmeasured, and the count under the period buttons says how many dominions are in that state. If that number is large, the tool has not been updating often enough.

The delta runs between the oldest and newest reading within the chosen window. If the only two updates are ten minutes apart you get a delta measured over ten minutes sitting under a heading that says 48h, sooo.... Span is the time between oldest and newest reading.

Why a dominion is missing from the list

  • Fewer than two readings in the period, as above.
  • It is a bot. Realm {{ vm.bot_realm }} is left out of all three tables. Since bots are not an attacking threat and will always follow a preconfigured plan it's better to not dilute the list: without the filter about 50% is bots.

The controls

Period
How far back to look: {{ vm.periods|join('h, ') }}h. The longer the period, the more they've been asleep.
Unchanged rows
How much of the Unchanged list to show, up to all of it. Top and Bottom are fixed at {{ vm.top_bottom_count }}.
Send to Discord
Posts all three lists to a configured webhook in secret.txt - if you've set that up. That report always covers {{ vm.discord_period }} hours and always sends {{ vm.top_bottom_count }} rows per list, whatever the page is set to.
